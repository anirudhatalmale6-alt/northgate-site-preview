About Northgate

Northgate Bookkeeping is a small firm. We chose to stay that way so every client gets consistent attention and a direct line to the person handling their file.

Most of our clients are owner-run businesses: contractors, restaurants, medical and dental practices, online sellers and consultants. Many came to us after a year of shoeboxes, spreadsheets, or a bookkeeper who stopped returning calls.

Who we work with

Businesses with anywhere from one owner to about thirty employees, plus freelancers and sole proprietors.

Software we use

QuickBooks Online, Xero and FreshBooks. If you already have a system, we'll work in it.

What working together looks like

We start with a call. It's free, it runs about half an hour, and you don't need to prepare anything. We ask about your business and look at what you have now.

You get a written quote. Monthly services have a flat fee based on how many transactions you have. One-time projects like cleanup and setup are priced up front. No hourly surprises.

We take it from there. You give us access to your bank feeds and documents through a secure portal. After that, expect a set of financial statements each month and a call whenever you need one.

Questions we hear a lot

Can you help if my books are months behind?

Yes. Catch-up work is common. We reconcile the backlog first, then move you to regular monthly service.

Is my information kept private?

Documents are shared through an encrypted portal, accounts use two-step sign-in, and only the people working on your file can open it.

Do I need to live nearby?

No. Most of our work is done online, and we're happy to meet in person when that's easier for you.
